## Listing

**Title:** WhatsApp Connect

**Short description (70 characters):** Send and receive WhatsApp messages from Frappe with the Meta Cloud API

**Long description:** WhatsApp Connect connects Frappe and ERPNext to the
WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta Cloud API), with no third-party gateway.
Send templates from any document with a live preview, reply within the
24-hour window from the timeline, automate notifications such as invoices
and payment receipts with the PDF attached, run campaigns with retries and
delivery reports, and collect answers with WhatsApp Flows. Opt-outs are
respected and webhooks are checked against your Meta App Secret.

**Categories:** Communication, Integrations
