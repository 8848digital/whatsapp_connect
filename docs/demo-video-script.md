# Demo video script (about 3 minutes)

Record on a test site with fake numbers, and a Meta test number connected
to a phone for the scenes where messages arrive.

| # | Time | Screen | What to show |
| - | ---- | ------ | ------------ |
| 1 | 0:00 | Logo, then the desk | "WhatsApp Connect: WhatsApp for Frappe and ERPNext through Meta's Cloud API." |
| 2 | 0:15 | WhatsApp Account | Credentials, App Secret, callback URL, Subscribe App to Webhooks. |
| 3 | 0:35 | WhatsApp Template | Type the text and watch the preview; Sync from Meta. |
| 4 | 1:00 | A Contact | ⋯ > Send WhatsApp, pick a template, preview, Send. |
| 5 | 1:25 | Phone, then the timeline | The customer replies; the reply shows in the timeline; Reply with free text. |
| 6 | 1:50 | WhatsApp Notification | Payment Entry, Submit, Send to the Party's Mobile Number, Attach Document Print; submit a payment and show the receipt on the phone. |
| 7 | 2:20 | WhatsApp Campaign | Recipient list, values, Send At; progress after sending. |
| 8 | 2:40 | WhatsApp Flow | Screens, Save to Meta, preview; the answers create a Lead. |
| 9 | 2:55 | WhatsApp Delivery Summary | Delivery and read rates by template. |

Cut pauses, blur any real number or token, and keep it under 4 minutes.
